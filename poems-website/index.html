🌸 poem one 🌸

"Watch Me Grow"

~ a voice for every girl ~

It's a festive atmosphere,

~ Cause there is a baby to be born;

Family members gathering and cheering

For arrival of new member of clan,

With sweets and whatnot at ready

Have thought the names already

Flowers on the gate!

And they no longer can wait

However,

That laughter and happiness fade

And all those colors lose their shades!

When it transpires to be a "Baby girl"

Tragedy of her life begins with her first breath

She hasn't even opened her eyes yet

And impose with all regret

Let me ask the world

Is that 'her fault'

'That she is born a girl'

Her tussle starts the day — she is born

With all the struggles along

Traditions continues to clip her wing

She is both a 'Me' and a 'We'

Her life revolves around —

Criticism, seal of approval, limit, suppress..

Isn't she have feelings to express

Or just an immortal without address

But —

She is some magic, some chaos and a bit of poetry

She is force of nature,

That comes into the world saying:

"WATCH ME GROW"!
